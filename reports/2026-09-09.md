> Research cutoff: 07:00 Asia/Shanghai. The latest completed sessions are September 8 for US and Hong Kong equities. This report does not use intraday prices as conclusions.

## Confirmed facts

1. **US market and macro risk** — On September 8, the S&P 500 fell 0.6% to 7,673.52, the Dow fell 1.2% to 52,786.07 and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.3% to 26,421.41. AP reported Brent crude briefly approached $99.50 per barrel and settled at $97.92.
2. **Hong Kong market** — The Hang Seng Index ended September 8 about 0.4% lower at 25,317; the Hang Seng Tech Index fell 1.6%. Tencent’s 8 September quote was HK$436.20, down 0.5%, while Pop Mart closed at HK$155.80, down 0.3%.
3. **Enterprise AI distribution** — Google Cloud and Accenture launched an Accenture Gemini Enterprise Business Group, with a planned 1,000-person forward-deployed-engineer workforce. This is an announced partnership, not yet a disclosed revenue contribution.
4. **Memory industry estimate** — TrendForce estimates 2Q26 DRAM revenue rose 59.5% QoQ to $154.73 billion, with supplier inventories at historic lows. It projects conventional-DRAM contract-price growth to slow to 13%–18% QoQ in 3Q26.

## Analysis and judgment

- **Energy has become a valuation input again.** Higher oil prices do not determine the Federal Reserve’s next decision, but they increase inflation uncertainty and can raise the hurdle for premium-duration equities.
- **The AI demand evidence has not broken.** The Google–Accenture partnership adds an enterprise-delivery channel, but it becomes investment evidence only if later adoption, cloud consumption or margin disclosure follows.
- **Memory remains a strong but cyclical exposure.** Low inventory and AI-server demand support current earnings power. Slower price gains, advanced-process migration, HBM yields and customer inventory are the appropriate next checks.

## Fixed watchlist

- **AAPL** — The scheduled September product event remains a catalyst, not evidence of an AI-led replacement cycle. Verify product eligibility, shipping and adoption only after disclosure.
- **GOOGL** — The new Accenture partnership supports distribution breadth, but does not alter the core test: enterprise adoption must translate into durable Cloud revenue and returns.
- **MSFT** — Enterprise distribution and Azure remain strengths. In the current risk regime, watch capex conversion and the sensitivity of the premium multiple to rates.
- **0700.HK** — Its one-day decline was smaller than the Hang Seng Tech move. The durable question remains whether AI infrastructure spending lifts ads, cloud and cash returns.
- **9992.HK** — The 0.3% move adds no thesis evidence. H1 overseas revenue and store productivity remain the decisive falsifiable checkpoints.
- **DRAM.A / Roundhill DRAM ETF context** — This remains actively managed global memory-equity exposure, not a spot-price index. The industry data support demand, while holdings, valuation, FX, capacity and cycle risk remain material.

## Watch next

- US inflation releases and whether energy-driven uncertainty persists in yields and premium technology valuations.
- Apple’s product announcements: separate features from commercial availability and observable adoption.
- Google Cloud disclosures that quantify Gemini Enterprise adoption or economics.
- Conventional DRAM price momentum, HBM qualification and customer inventory through 3Q26.
