## Analysis and judgment

- **The risk signal is macro-led, not company-specific.** Higher oil and a 4.95% 10-year yield increase the discount-rate challenge for premium technology valuations. They do not alone establish a future Fed decision or an earnings outcome.
- **Apple’s product launch has moved from announcement to measurable tests.** A third-party shipment estimate supplies a scenario, not a conclusion. Preorder demand, availability, mix and Apple’s later gross-margin commentary are the appropriate evidence.
- **Memory requires separation of markets.** A soft single spot session does not overturn the prior evidence on AI-server/HBM and contract demand. Track contract prices, inventory, HBM qualifications, capacity and DRAM ETF holdings rather than treating one quote as a sector index.
