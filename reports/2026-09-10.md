## Confirmed facts

1. **US market and macro risk** — On September 9, the S&P 500 fell 0.5% to 7,636.36, the Dow fell 0.8% to 52,380.66 and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.6% to 26,253.34. AP reported Brent crude rose 3.4% back above $100 per barrel. The 10-year Treasury yield ended at 4.84%, up from 4.80% late Tuesday.
2. **Hong Kong market** — The Hang Seng Index closed 42 points lower at 25,274 and the Hang Seng Tech Index fell 0.76% to 4,420 on September 9. This is an index-level market fact, not company-specific evidence for Tencent or Pop Mart.
3. **Apple product and AI disclosure** — Apple introduced iPhone Duo, its first foldable iPhone, powered by A20 Pro. Apple states that the device supports Apple Intelligence and beta Siri AI, with preorders beginning October 16 and availability beginning October 23.
4. **Memory spot market** — TrendForce reports mainstream DDR4 1Gx8 3200 spot pricing rose 1.78% week over week to $45.36 on September 8. It also reports limited DDR5 procurement and a 2.71% weekly decline in 512Gb TLC-wafer spot prices.
5. **Microsoft AI governance** — Microsoft, AFT and UFT announced a National AI Safety & Privacy Standard for schools. Microsoft says districts can incorporate protections into customer agreements; this is a policy and procurement framework, not a disclosed AI-revenue contribution.

## Analysis and judgment

- **Macro has moved ahead of the company news flow.** Oil above $100 and the increase in long yields heighten discount-rate sensitivity for premium technology valuations. Neither observation establishes a future Federal Reserve action.
- **Apple’s launch changes the evidence set, not yet the earnings model.** A first foldable iPhone and AI-function rollout create observable tests: preorder demand, supply availability, mix, regional eligibility and later gross-margin commentary.
- **Memory needs granular tracking.** DDR4 strength alongside limited DDR5 and weak NAND activity cautions against treating the sector as a single price curve. HBM/server demand, contract prices, inventory and the ETF’s actual holdings remain more decision-relevant than one spot quote.

## Fixed watchlist

- **AAPL** — The iPhone Duo disclosure confirms a new hardware form factor and AI software path. Track October demand and post-launch mix without assuming a replacement-cycle uplift.
- **GOOGL** — The prior Gemini Enterprise delivery announcement remains relevant. The next material proof is attributable Cloud consumption, revenue or returns rather than another partnership headline.
- **MSFT** — The school AI-safety standard may lower a governance barrier in one customer segment, but it does not quantify Azure, Copilot or margin impact. Continue to track enterprise monetization and capex conversion.
- **0700.HK** — The tech-index decline supplies no new Tencent operating evidence. Watch whether AI infrastructure spending improves advertising, cloud and cash returns.
- **9992.HK** — The broad risk-off session adds no thesis evidence. Overseas revenue recovery, store productivity and IP breadth remain the falsifiable checkpoints.
