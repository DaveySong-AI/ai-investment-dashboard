## Analysis and judgment

- **The US rebound is a repricing event, not a macro all-clear.** Oil’s pullback and CPI data near expectations helped equities end a four-day slide, but the 10-year yield still edged higher. A single session does not settle the path for inflation, policy or technology multiples.
- **Cross-market risk appetite remains uneven.** Hong Kong’s fifth consecutive loss and low volumes contrast with the US recovery. That divergence supports a cautious, evidence-first posture; it does not establish a market trend or a company-specific operating change.
- **Memory needs workload and market separation.** The SK hynix discussion is useful context for the importance of bandwidth and capacity across AI systems. It does not overturn the need to verify HBM qualification, contract pricing, inventories, bit supply and actual DRAM ETF holdings. Mixed one-day spot quotes remain a granular input, not a cycle call.
