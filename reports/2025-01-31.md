# AI Investment Morning Brief — 2025-01-31

> Historical demonstration snapshot. Data reflects the US close on 2025-01-31 and is not live.

## Market view

AI spending meets a higher bar. Large technology companies continued to invest, while investors increasingly demanded a visible path from capital expenditure to revenue and cash flow.

## What mattered

1. **Policy patience** — The Federal Reserve maintained the federal funds target at 4.25–4.50%.
2. **AI capex scrutiny** — The conversation shifted from how much companies spend to what they earn from it.
3. **Earnings dispersion** — Execution quality mattered more than broad sector exposure.
