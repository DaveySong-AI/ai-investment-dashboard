# AI Investment Research Update — 2026-09-07

> Cross-market update. NYSE is closed for Labor Day, so this report deliberately does not present US closing prices as if they were current.

## View

The latest earnings disclosures support the AI investment case at the revenue level. They do not settle the valuation question. The work now is to track the conversion from AI demand and capacity spend into durable margin and free cash flow.

## What changed

1. **Apple** — FY26 Q3 revenue was $109.4 billion, up 16% year over year; iPhone, Mac and Services set June-quarter records. The key follow-up is whether new Siri AI capabilities change upgrade or services economics.
2. **Alphabet** — Q2 revenue was $119.8 billion, up 24%; Google Cloud revenue rose 82% to $24.8 billion, driven by enterprise AI infrastructure and solutions. This is the strongest direct evidence in the watchlist that AI infrastructure demand is monetizing.
3. **Microsoft** — FY26 Q4 revenue reached $90.0 billion, up 18%. The thesis remains distribution plus Azure, with capital intensity and return on investment as the guardrails.
4. **Tencent** — 2Q26 revenue rose 11% to RMB204.8 billion; Marketing Services grew 22%, while capital expenditure rose 176% to RMB52.8 billion. AI-enhanced ads and cloud demand support the upside case, but compute procurement and cash conversion now deserve equal attention.
5. **Pop Mart** — Keep the thesis on evidence: global IP breadth, overseas repeatability and channel productivity. Avoid upgrading a consumer-IP thesis solely from momentum or a single character cycle.

## Watch next

- The first US session after Labor Day: does price action validate or challenge the current earnings narrative?
- Cloud demand versus incremental capex: GOOGL, MSFT and 0700.HK.
- Apple product and AI adoption signals ahead of the next earnings cycle.
